# 🐾 PawCare — Pet Care Services Portal

A modern and user-friendly website designed to help pet owners
organise their pets' care in one place.

PawCare brings together pet profiles, health records, nutrition
planning, grooming routines, training activities, adoption guidance,
pet products and service request forms.

## 🌐 Live Website

Visit PawCare here:

https://praharshita08-beep.github.io/Pet-care-system-portal/

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of PawCare is to create a convenient digital
platform where pet owners can organise everyday pet-care activities.

The website aims to make pet care easier by bringing useful tools
and information together in one accessible interface.

## ✨ Features

### 1. Pet Profile Management
- Add pet profiles.
- Edit pet information.
- Delete pet profiles.
- Record pet type, breed, date of birth and notes.
- Reuse saved pet details in care forms.

### 2. Health and Vaccination Tracker
- Add health and vaccination records.
- Record veterinary visits and medication notes.
- View and delete saved records.
- Organise important health information.

### 3. Food and Nutrition Planner
- Create a feeding checklist.
- Record food preferences and notes.
- Mark meals as completed.

### 4. Grooming and Hygiene
- Interactive grooming checklist.
- Completion progress indicator.
- Reset the checklist when needed.
- Grooming service request option.

### 5. Training and Exercise
- Generate activity session ideas.
- Choose movement, enrichment, training or calm-time activities.
- Select the available session duration.

### 6. Adoption and Rescue Centre
- Provides responsible adoption guidance.
- Includes an adoption enquiry checklist.
- Helps prospective pet owners consider the responsibilities
  of adopting an animal.

### 7. Pet Departmental Store
- Browse sample pet products.
- Filter by product category.
- Search for products.
- Add products to the shopping cart.
- Change quantities and view the subtotal.

### 8. Pet Care Service Requests
- Select a pet and service.
- Enter contact details and a preferred date.
- Prepare a demonstration service request confirmation.

## 💾 Data Storage and Limitations

PawCare is currently a static frontend project.

- Pet profiles and health records are stored in the browser.
- Browser-stored data is not automatically shared between users
  or devices.
- Nutrition plans and shopping cart contents are demonstration
  interactions and are not stored in a central database.
- Booking and adoption forms display demonstration confirmations;
  they do not send requests to real service providers.
- The pet store uses sample products and does not process payments.
- The website does not replace professional veterinary advice.

## 🔒 Privacy and Responsible Use

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information.
Browser data may be lost if the browser's stored site data is cleared.

Health information and care recommendations should be reviewed with
a qualified veterinarian when appropriate.
